User Manual — Nominations
Nominating for an RC Raceday event is simple, but there are a few rules depending on the event setup.
This guide explains how to nominate, how class limits work, how preferences work, how pricing is calculated, and what happens if you nominate late.

1. What Is a Nomination?
A nomination is your official entry into an event.
When you nominate, you choose:

The day you want to race (if multi‑day)

The classes you want to enter

Any preferences (if allowed)

Your merchandise options (if required)

Your nomination determines your race schedule, your pricing, and your entry into the event.

2. How to Nominate
Step‑by‑Step
Open the event page

Select the day you want to race (if multi‑day)

Choose your classes

Choose preferences (if allowed)

Review your pricing

Add merchandise (if required)

Confirm your nomination

Pay (if applicable)

Once confirmed, your nomination is saved and visible to event organisers.

3. Class Selection
Available Classes
You will only see classes that:

Are allowed at the track

Are assigned to the event

Are assigned to the specific day

Are available for nomination

If a class is missing, it is not offered for that event or day.

Max Classes Per Driver
Each event sets a limit on how many classes you can enter.

Example:

Code
Max Classes Per Driver: 2
You cannot select more than this number.

4. Preferences
Some events allow you to select preference classes.

Preferences help organisers place drivers when entries are limited or when balancing heats.

Preferences Allowed
If enabled, you will see a toggle or option to mark a class as a preference.

Preference Limit
Events may set a limit on how many preferences you can choose.

Example:

Code
Preference Limit: 1
You can only mark one class as your preferred choice.

Do Preferences Affect Pricing?
It depends on the event:

Some events charge for preferences

Some events make preferences free

Some pricing modes ignore preferences entirely

The event page will show your total before you confirm.

5. Pricing
Your nomination cost depends on:

Your membership type

The event’s pricing mode

The classes you select

Whether preferences are charged

Whether you nominate late

Pricing Modes
Per Entry Pricing
You pay one fee, no matter how many classes you enter.

Tiered Pricing
You pay:

A first‑class price

A cheaper additional‑class price

Per‑Class Pricing
Each class has its own price.

Late Fees
If you nominate after the late‑entry activation time, a late fee may be added.

The event page will show:

Whether late entries are allowed

Whether a late fee applies

Your final total

6. Late Entries
Late Entries Enabled
If late entries are enabled, you can nominate after the normal close time.

A late fee may apply.

Late Entries Disabled
If late entries are disabled, you cannot nominate after nominations close.

Late Entry Window
Events may define:

When late entries begin

When late entries close

If you are inside the late‑entry window, the system will show:

Code
Late Entry — Fee Applied
7. Changing Your Nomination
You can change your nomination until nominations close.

You may update:

Classes

Preferences

Merchandise

Personal details

After nominations close:

Changes may not be allowed

Late entry rules apply

A late fee may apply

8. Common Questions
Can I nominate for multiple days?
Yes, if the event allows multi‑day nominations.

Can I nominate for more classes than the limit?
No — the system will prevent it.

Can I remove a class after nominating?
Yes, until nominations close.

Why don’t I see all classes?
Only classes allowed at the track and assigned to the event will appear.

Do preferences guarantee I get that class?
No — they help organisers prioritise entries.

Do preferences cost extra?
Some events charge for preferences depending on pricing mode.

Can I nominate late?
Only if the event allows late entries.

9. Summary
Nominations let you enter classes for an event

You can only select classes available for that day

Class limits and preference limits may apply

Pricing depends on membership, classes, preferences, and late fees

You can change your nomination until nominations close

Late entries may be allowed, but may include a fee

This system ensures fair, organised, and flexible nominations for all drivers.
