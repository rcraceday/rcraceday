User Pricing Manual
This guide explains how event pricing works when you nominate for an RC Raceday event.
Different events use different pricing modes, and each mode calculates your entry fee in a different way.

Pricing is based on:

Your membership type

The classes you nominate for

Whether the event uses Per Entry, Tiered, or Per‑Class pricing

Whether you nominate late

Whether the event charges for preferences

Whether any classes are marked free

This manual explains each pricing mode and how your total is calculated.

1. Membership Types
Your pricing depends on your membership category:

Member

Non‑Member

Junior

Each pricing mode has different prices for each membership type.

2. Pricing Modes
Events use one of three pricing modes.
Only one mode is active for each event.

A. Per Entry Pricing (Default)
Per Entry Pricing charges one single entry fee, no matter how many classes you nominate for.

What you pay
You pay one fee based on your membership type:

Member Entry Fee

Non‑Member Entry Fee

Junior Entry Fee

Preferences
If the event does not charge for preferences, preference classes are free.

If the event does charge for preferences, preference classes are treated like normal classes.

Free Entry
If the event is marked Free Entry, your total cost is $0.

B. Tiered Pricing
Tiered Pricing charges:

A First Class price

An Additional Class/es price

Each membership type has its own tiered prices.

Example
If you nominate for 3 classes:

First class → $20

Additional classes → $10 each

Total = $40

Preferences
If the event does not charge for preferences, preference classes are free and do not count toward class totals.

If the event does charge for preferences, preference classes count as normal classes.

C. Per‑Class Pricing
Per‑Class Pricing gives each class its own price:

Member price

Non‑Member price

Junior price

Free toggle

Example
If you nominate for:

Class A → Member price $25

Class B → Free

Class C → Member price $15

Total = $40

Preferences
If the event does not charge for preferences, preference classes are free.

If the event does charge for preferences, preference classes use the per‑class price.

3. Late Entries & Late Fees
Some events allow late entries after nominations close.

Late Entries Enabled
If late entries are enabled, you may nominate after the normal close time.

Late entries begin at:

The event’s Late Fee Activation time

Or immediately after nominations close (if no activation time is set)

Late entries may close at:

The event’s Late Entries Close time

Or remain open indefinitely (if no close time is set)

Late Fee
If you nominate during the late‑entry window, a Late Fee is added to your total.

Late Entries Disabled
If late entries are disabled, you cannot nominate after nominations close.

4. Free Classes
Some classes may be marked Free.

If a class is free:

You pay $0 for that class

It does not count toward tiered pricing

It does not affect per‑entry pricing

It does not affect late fees

5. How Your Total Is Calculated
Your total is calculated using:

Your membership type

The event’s pricing mode

The classes you nominate for

Whether any classes are free

Whether preferences are charged

Whether you are nominating late

Whether a late fee applies

The system automatically calculates your total and shows it before you confirm your nomination.

7. Summary
Events choose one pricing mode

Membership type affects pricing

Preferences may be free or charged

Free classes cost $0

Late entries may add a late fee

Your total is always shown before you confirm your nomination

This system ensures pricing is fair, flexible, and consistent across all RC Raceday events.
